# Mathematics: Algebra and Geometry Basics

## Quadratic equations

A quadratic equation has the form ax^2 + bx + c = 0, where a is not zero. Its solutions are given by
the quadratic formula: x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / (2a).

The expression b^2 - 4ac is called the discriminant. If it is positive, the equation has two
different real solutions. If it is zero, there is exactly one real solution. If it is negative,
there are no real solutions.

## Triangles

The angles in any triangle add up to 180 degrees. In a right-angled triangle, the Pythagorean
theorem says that a^2 + b^2 = c^2, where c is the hypotenuse, the side opposite the right angle.
The area of a triangle is half the base times the height.

## Circles

The circumference of a circle is 2 * pi * r, where r is the radius. The area of a circle is
pi * r^2. The diameter is twice the radius. The number pi is about 3.14159 and is the ratio of a
circle's circumference to its diameter.
