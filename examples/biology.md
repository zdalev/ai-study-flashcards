# Biology: Cells and Photosynthesis

## The cell

All living things are made of cells. The cell is the basic unit of life. Cells come from other
cells, and they carry out the processes that keep an organism alive.

Cells fall into two groups. Prokaryotic cells, such as bacteria, have no nucleus; their DNA floats
freely in the cytoplasm. Eukaryotic cells, found in plants, animals and fungi, keep their DNA inside
a nucleus that is surrounded by a membrane.

## Organelles

The mitochondria release energy from glucose in a process called cellular respiration, so they are
often called the powerhouses of the cell. Ribosomes build proteins. The cell membrane controls what
enters and leaves the cell. Plant cells also have a rigid cell wall made of cellulose, and
chloroplasts that contain the green pigment chlorophyll.

## Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplasts of plant cells. Plants use light energy to turn
carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. The word equation is: carbon dioxide + water ->
glucose + oxygen (light energy is needed). Chlorophyll absorbs the light. The oxygen is released
through tiny pores in the leaves called stomata.
